Selection

How these papers were picked

Thousands of AI papers appear on arXiv each month. A paper made this list if it passed at least one of these tests. The meters on each deck (impact, new idea, momentum) are an editorial judgement, not a citation count.

It changed what everyone builds

Transformers, RLHF, LoRA, FlashAttention and vLLM are inside almost every production model and serving stack. You can't follow the field without them.

It introduced a pattern others copied

Chain-of-thought, ReAct, test-time compute and GRPO-style reasoning RL each started a new line of work within months of release.

It's rising fast right now

2026 papers that the community is upvoting and building on: hybrid Mamba–Transformer models, attention over depth, next-concept prediction, and agents that improve their own harness.

It explains something surprising

Interpretability work that shows what's actually happening inside, such as monosemantic features, attention sinks and massive activations.
